The project

Lab results,
without the lab wait.

Checking milk quality by hand is slow and depends on who's doing it. Qualify Milk learns from graded lab samples and grades new ones instantly.

What it does

Dairy producers and quality-control teams measure seven properties of a sample. The model compares those readings to {{ '{:,}'.format(metrics.dataset.rows) if metrics else '1,059' }} graded samples and predicts whether the milk is high, medium or low quality, with a confidence score and a note on any reading that looks off.

Ten algorithms were compared, from Logistic Regression to a stacked ensemble. Each one was scored on samples it had never seen, because the dataset contains many repeated rows that make normal accuracy look better than it is.
