Why two scores? The dataset has {{ '{:,}'.format(metrics.dataset.rows) }} rows but only {{ metrics.dataset.unique_samples }} unique samples. The other {{ metrics.dataset.duplicates }} rows are exact repeats. With a normal random split, copies of the same sample land in both training and test data, so most models score about 99%. The unseen score keeps every copy together, so the model is always tested on samples it has never seen. That's the honest measure of how it will do on new milk, and it's how the best model was chosen.
