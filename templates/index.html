These are the inputs the model was trained on, with the range it has seen for each one.

TasteGood / Bad

Whether the sample tastes normal.

OdorGood / Bad

Whether it smells normal.

FatOptimal / Low

Whether fat content meets the optimal level.

TurbidityClear / Cloudy

Cloudiness from suspended particles.

Colour240 – 255

Whiteness on a 255 scale. Lower is more yellow.
